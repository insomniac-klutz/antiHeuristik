# Ambiguity Scoping

Practice for: "We want to add AI to our product. What do you do?"

## The Framework
1. **What problem are we solving?** (not "what AI can we add")
2. **Who has the problem?** (user segment, frequency, pain level)
3. **What's the simplest version?** (MVP that proves value)
4. **What do I say no to?** (scope control)
5. **How do we measure success?** (before writing code)

## Scenarios to Practice
1. "We're a legal tech company. Add AI." — scope to document review? contract analysis? research?
2. "Our support costs are too high. Use AI." — bot? agent assist? ticket routing? knowledge base?
3. "We want a copilot for our users." — what actions? what data? what guardrails?
4. "Our competitors have AI features." — do we need AI or do we need better UX?

*(Practice each: first question you'd ask, what you'd propose as MVP, what you'd explicitly cut)*
