# scopeCreep

> 5 companies want AI. maybe 2 should get it.

not a codebase — a structured decision exercise. take 5 real-world scenarios, produce a 1-page scoping doc for each, then build the ONE that actually makes sense. the hardest skill in applied AI is saying "don't use AI."

## the 5 scenarios

- **A: logistics route optimization** — "our competitors have AI." cool. is AI actually the gap, or is it better data/UX?
- **B: legal contract analysis** — "flag risky clauses." this IS a real AI problem. scope the MVP
- **C: e-commerce conversion boosting** — "add AI to increase conversion." 50 possible features. which ONE first?
- **D: internal knowledge base chatbot** — everyone wants this. most implementations suck. why?
- **E: customer support cost reduction** — "$2M/year in support. cut it in half." clear objective, multiple approaches

## what each scoping doc must contain

1. problem definition (what's actually being solved, not what was asked)
2. proposed approach (including "don't use AI" if that's the answer)
3. why this approach over alternatives
4. MVP definition (smallest thing that proves value)
5. what you're explicitly cutting and why
6. cost estimate (build + run)
7. risk assessment
8. success metrics (before writing a line of code)

## what will break you

- **saying no**: recommending against AI when the stakeholder wants AI
- **ambiguity navigation**: scoping from a vague request with missing context
- **"our competitors have AI"**: figuring out if the AI is actually their advantage
- **prioritization**: 50 possible features, incomplete data, one shot
- **cost modeling from scratch**: estimating a new system, not optimizing an existing one

## why this matters

the hardest skill in applied AI isn't building — it's deciding what to build. saying "don't use AI" when AI isn't the answer is a muscle you only develop by working through ambiguous asks.

## stretch

- present each scoping doc, get pushback, revise
- "6 months later" retrospective for each scenario
- for "don't use AI" scenarios, propose the non-AI solution and estimate impact
